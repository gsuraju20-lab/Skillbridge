How it works

Six months from graduate to hireable

Every learner follows the same path. You study online, practise on real briefs, and finish with proof of what you can do.

  1. Months 1 and 2

    Learn the foundations

    Short lessons and weekly exercises on social media, content, and how customers find businesses online.

  2. Months 3 and 4

    Practise on real briefs

    Build campaigns for small businesses and create a portfolio you can show any employer.

  3. Months 5 and 6

    Get certified and matched

    Pass the final assessment, receive your certificate, and be introduced to employers hiring for marketing roles.

Learners who move quickly can finish in three months.

The first track

Digital marketing and social media

Almost every business now needs someone who can run its online presence, and much of the work can be done remotely.

What you will be able to do

  • Plan and schedule a month of social media posts
  • Write captions and short copy that sell
  • Design simple graphics and edit short videos on a phone
  • Run a small paid ad campaign and read the results
  • Reply to customers and grow an online community
  • Report to a business owner in plain language

Roles this prepares you for

Social media manager Content creator Community manager Paid ads assistant Email marketing assistant Freelance marketer
